No more old "Sold ..." pop-ups every time you open the app. Alerts that arrive while the app is opening go straight to the Alerts tab.
Swipe an alert away (left, right or up). Tap it to see everything: buy or sell first, entry, stop-loss, targets, shares, risk, score and why it came.
The Alerts tab now lists every notification. "Clear all" asks first and removes them for good - cleared or dismissed alerts do not come back.
Every stock page shows Support & resistance: Resistance 2 and 1, the live price, Support 1 and 2 and the major support.
